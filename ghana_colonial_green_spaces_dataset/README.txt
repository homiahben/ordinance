Ghana Colonial Green Spaces Dataset Starter, 1876-1957

Files:
- green_spaces.csv: GIS-ready table of sites/green spaces.
- events.csv: laws, protests, reports, reserve creation events, amendments.
- purpose_categories.csv: color codes for mapping.
- sources.csv: source register.
- green_spaces_points.geojson: GeoJSON points for records with coordinates.
- mapping_in_R.R: R script for Leaflet and ggplot2.
- advanced_research_prompt.txt: reusable research prompt.

Important:
This is a starter dataset, not a final comprehensive gazetteer. Exact reserve coordinates should be replaced with official polygons from WDPA/Protected Planet, Ghana Forestry Commission, Lands Commission, colonial gazettes, or archival survey maps. Rows with blank coordinates are intentionally marked as needing verification.
